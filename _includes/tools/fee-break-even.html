Account fee break-even calculator

How much of your interest the fees take, and the balance below which the account loses money. Runs in your browser only.

Interest is figured as simple interest (balance × APY) so that it sits on the same basis as the fees. Because APY is already the effective annual figure, balance times APY is the full year's interest; compounding it again would double-count. The math also assumes the balance holds steady for the full year; in practice each monthly fee comes out of principal, so the drag builds a little faster than shown. Enter 0 for a waivable fee only if you are confident you will meet the waiver condition every cycle — miss one month and that month is charged in full.
