Early withdrawal break-even calculator

Should you break this CD and move the money? Runs in your browser only.

Disclosures usually state the penalty as a number of days of interest, so enter it that way — 6 months is 180 days. Interest you have earned is compounded from the APY you enter. The penalty is charged as simple interest at the stated rate rather than the APY, so this converts your APY back to that rate before applying it — otherwise the estimate would overstate what your bank actually deducts. If the penalty is larger than the interest earned so far, the difference comes out of principal, which federal rules permit. Check your own disclosure for the exact formula and any grace-period exception.
