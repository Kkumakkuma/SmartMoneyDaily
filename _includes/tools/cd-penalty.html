Early withdrawal break-even calculator

Should you break this CD and move the money? Runs in your browser only.

Enter the interest rate printed on your disclosure, not the APY. Disclosures set the penalty as a fixed number of months of simple interest at that rate, charged whether or not you have earned that much — so this tool applies the penalty as simple interest and the interest you keep as compounded growth, which is how the two are actually calculated. If earned interest is smaller than the penalty, the difference comes out of principal, which federal rules permit. Check your own disclosure for the exact formula and any grace-period exception.
