Fix It or Float It Calculator

Lock the money into a CD, or leave it in savings? Runs in your browser only.

The three paths are not forecasts. They draw out the assumption you typed in, and nothing guarantees that rates move by the same amount every quarter — in practice no one knows the path in advance. Each quarter is compounded at that quarter's APY, while most banks compound daily, so the totals here can sit a few dollars away from a statement. The asymmetry that matters is this: the CD rate is fixed by contract for the full term, while the floating rate can be changed at any time, including between the quarters modeled here. The cost of breaking a CD early is not calculated on this page — use the CD early withdrawal penalty calculator for that. Taxes are not included.
